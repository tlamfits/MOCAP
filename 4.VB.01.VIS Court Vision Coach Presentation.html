How it works

Two planes, one feedback loop

Overhead cameras own where everyone is. End-line cameras own what happens at the net. Together they triangulate the ball in 3D.

Tier 2 build: OH-1/OH-2 at 26–30 ft ceiling; EL-1/EL-2 at 10–15 ft behind baselines. Stereo overlap triangulates ball position within 15 cm.

Floor plane · Overhead

  • X·Y position for all 12 players
  • Formations, seams & coverage maps
  • Movement paths & jump counts

Net plane · End-line

  • Contact & block-hand heights
  • Attack direction & set tempo
  • Serve trajectories & jersey IDs
