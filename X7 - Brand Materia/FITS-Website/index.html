Governing Principles

The Science Behind Every Decision

01

"If You Can't, You Won't"

Hardware limitations prevent movement solutions. If the range, strength, or structure isn't there — the movement simply cannot happen.

02

"Just Because You Can, Doesn't Mean You Will"

Having the physical capacity doesn't guarantee the nervous system will use it. Software limitations prevent expression of existing capability.

03

"It's Not the Parts, But How They Work Together"

Integration and coordination are the bridge. The magic is in how 12 components synchronize under competitive demands.
